Research build
Important: this build does not claim to reproduce the complete LOWKEY FX A.P.A. method. Public material located describes the method at a high level and promotes mentorship for full training; the exact entry, invalidation and management rules are not publicly documented in enough detail to encode faithfully. The SMC labels below are explicitly provisional, rule-based chart interpretations, not proof of institutional orders or a validated profitable edge.

Validation checklist before live use: (1) confirm candle source, symbol definition, timestamps and spread; (2) compare marked swings, sweeps, BOS/CHoCH and zones with manually annotated examples; (3) prevent look-ahead bias by using only confirmed candles; (4) test unseen periods with fees/spread/slippage; (5) paper-trade and record every discrepancy. No win-rate or profit claim is made. Do not copy a candidate into a real account until you have independently verified it.

Connection & privacy settings
Configured to request the documented Biquote REST API. The execution environment cannot reach the API directly, so use Test feed in your browser before trusting prices.

This is a single-file app, not a secure account system. Candles are cached in IndexedDB and settings/journal in local browser storage. Requests go to the market-data provider and chart CDN; they may receive ordinary connection metadata. Do not store brokerage passwords here. The strategy detectors are heuristic and no order is placed or executed.
