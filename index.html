Validation checklist before live use: (1) confirm candle source, symbol definition, timestamps and spread; (2) compare marked swings, sweeps, BOS/CHoCH and zones with manually annotated examples; (3) prevent look-ahead bias by using only confirmed candles; (4) test unseen periods with fees/spread/slippage; (5) paper-trade and record every discrepancy. No win-rate or profit claim is made. Do not copy a candidate into a real account until you have independently verified it.

Variable registry

Every variable must define its name, formula, unit, lookback, allowable range, default, data source, and the earliest candle/time when it becomes knowable.

Examples to formalise: swing strength, sweep depth, FVG size, displacement threshold, volatility regime, session, spread, higher-timeframe bias.

Evidence dashboard

Required outputs: trade count, coverage dates, net expectancy in R, win rate, profit factor, drawdown, costs, losing streaks, walk-forward folds, untouched holdout, and performance by market regime.

Explanation timeline

For each hypothetical trade, show which conditions were true, which failed, the values used, the entry/stop/target calculation, and why a candidate was accepted or rejected.

Research lifecycleNo shortcut from backtest to live
1
Propose and explain a hypothesis
State the market rationale, feature combination, entry, exit, invalidation, and failure cases before looking at the result.
2
Freeze rules and variables
Version the exact formulas, parameters, data assumptions, and source code. Add unit tests and no-look-ahead checks.
3
Test chronologically with realistic costs
Use spread, fees, slippage, gaps, delayed fills, and conservative same-candle stop/target handling. Compare with simple baselines.
4
Walk-forward, untouched holdout, and robustness
Record every tested combination. Test unseen periods and regimes; reject fragile results or insufficient samples.
5
Forward paper test, then human review
Freeze the candidate and log time-stamped paper signals. No real-money automation and no claim of guaranteed future performance.

Connection & privacy settings
Configured to request the documented Biquote REST API. The execution environment cannot reach the API directly, so use Test feed in your browser before trusting prices.
Use a deployed Cloudflare Worker from the repository's proxy folder if Binance returns HTTP 451. Leave blank to try Binance directly.

This is a single-file app, not a secure account system. Candles are cached in IndexedDB and settings/journal in local browser storage. Requests go to the market-data provider and chart CDN; they may receive ordinary connection metadata. Do not store brokerage passwords here. The strategy detectors are heuristic and no order is placed or executed.
